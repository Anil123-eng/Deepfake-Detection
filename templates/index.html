How It Works

Our hybrid architecture analyzes both individual frames and their temporal relationships.

01
🎞️

Frame Extraction

Each video is sampled into 25 evenly-spaced frames and resized to 224×224.

02
🖼️

Facial Cropping

Faces within frames are detected and isolated, focusing the CNN on manipulated regions.

03
🧠

CNN Feature Extraction

An EfficientNetB0 CNN extracts high-level spatial features from every frame.

04
📈

RNN Temporal Analysis

LSTM layers learn blink patterns, micro-expressions and frame artifacts across time.
